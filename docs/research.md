# Thoracic Rotation Exercises — Research & Video Links

Source doc: [Thoracic Rotation Exercises — Research & Video Links](https://claude.ai/artifact/Sw7u4S7gFiMf5fBjSR9YSi)

Two separate exercises in the app:

- **Exercise A — Kneeling Back Rotation Stretch (straight-arm reach)** (`src/exercises/kneeling-back-rotation-stretch.json`): from hands and knees, sweep one arm straight up toward the ceiling while the upper back rotates.
- **Exercise B — Quadruped Thoracic Rotation (hand behind head)** (`src/exercises/quadruped-thoracic-rotation.json`): sit the hips back toward the heels to lock the lower back, put one hand behind the head, bring the elbow down toward the opposite hand, then rotate up.

## 1. Scientific studies

- [Comparative Analysis of Thoracic Rotation Exercises: Standing vs. Quadruped (PubMed, 2024)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38902213/). 39 healthy adults did a quadruped or a standing thoracic rotation exercise for 2 weeks. Both improved thoracic rotation by the same amount.
- [Same study, summary page (Waseda University)](https://waseda.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/comparative-analysis-of-thoracic-rotation-exercises-range-of-moti/)
- [Reliability of thoracic spine rotation measurements in healthy adults (Johnson et al., 2012, J Athl Train)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3418115). The lumbar-locked test (Exercise B's position) averaged about 41° of rotation per side.
- [Visual estimation of thorax rotation in the quadruped lumbar-locked position (Whetstone & Matsel, 2024, IJSPT)](https://ijspt.scholasticahq.com/article/116154). Counts about 50° or more as unrestricted rotation.
- [Thoracic spine exercise prescription in sport: systematic review (2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7173996/). Caveat: no studies yet test whether thoracic exercises improve mobility in athletes specifically.

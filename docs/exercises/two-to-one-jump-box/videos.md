## Pattern clarification

- **Takeoff mechanics:** stand facing the box, feet about hip-width. Dip into a quarter squat (hips back, knees bent) while swinging the arms back, then jump explosively off **both feet** together, swinging the arms forward and up. Both feet leave the floor at the same time; this is not a run-up or a split-stance push.
- **Box height:** neither GymVisual nor the sources give a height. Will Levy's description stresses mechanics over height, and a single-leg landing is harder than the standard two-foot box jump, so a low-to-moderate box is appropriate. Suggestion for the animation (not from a source): a box around mid-shin to knee height (roughly 30 to 45 cm / 12 to 18 in), so the landing knee bend reads clearly.
- **Single-leg landing position:** land on **one foot** in the middle of the box top (author the right leg; the Left/Right switch mirrors it). Land softly, ball of the foot to heel, with the landing knee bent and tracking over the toes (cue from SHRED: knee toward the pinky toe, not caving in), hips back and chest slightly forward. The other leg stays off the box, bent and held behind or just below the body. Arms stay forward for balance. Hold the landing briefly (Resilient Human suggests 1 to 2 seconds) before moving.
- **Stepping down:** after the hold, stand tall on the box, place the free foot down, then **step** (do not jump) back down to the floor and reset facing the box. Will Levy's description says to step down rather than jump down, to avoid unnecessary impact.

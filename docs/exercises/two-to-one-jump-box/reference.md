## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** standing tall facing the box (side view), feet hip-width.
2. **Countermovement:** dip into a quarter squat with arms swinging back (two-foot loading).
3. **Takeoff:** **two-foot** explosive jump, arms swinging forward.
4. **Landing:** **single-leg** landing on the box: lead leg bent (knee over toes, hip back), trail leg held behind/below, arms forward for balance.
5. **Return:** stand up, step down, reset.

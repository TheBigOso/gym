## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** high plank. Side view.
2. **Phase 1:** hips lift up and back into downward-facing dog: arms long, heels reaching toward the floor, spine long.
3. **Phase 2:** raise one leg straight up and back (three-legged dog), keeping hips square, then lower it.
4. **Return:** back through down dog to plank.
5. **Highlights:** hamstrings and calves of the standing leg, glutes and hamstrings of the lifted leg, shoulders.

## Notes for the video, research and integrate tickets

Hold vs flow: the reference flows plank to dog to leg lift. Research ticket should give typical hold times for the leg-lift position.

## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Setup:** four small cones in a line beside the athlete.
2. **Stance:** balance on one leg, other knee bent with the foot lifted behind/beside the support leg, arms at the sides or slightly forward.
3. **Movement:** single-leg lateral hops over each cone in turn, landing softly on the same foot, sticking briefly, then the next hop.
4. **Path:** lateral travel down the line of cones; the 35 s video likely returns and switches legs.
5. **Highlights:** quadriceps, glutes and calves of the hopping leg.

## Notes for the video, research and integrate tickets

Confirm cone spacing, whether the athlete returns in the opposite direction, and when legs switch.

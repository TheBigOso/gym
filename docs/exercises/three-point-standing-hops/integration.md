# 3 Point Standing Hops: integration notes

Issue #39 (Batch 1, exercise 8 of 10). Movement reference: GymVisual #768012 (see `reference.md`). Built 2026-11-26.

Files: `build/meta/three-point-standing-hops.json`, `build/author-three-point-standing-hops.js`, `src/exercises/three-point-standing-hops.json`, `exports/three-point-standing-hops.glb` (two clips, `_right` and `_left`, 6 s at 30 fps).

## What was built

**Layout.** Four flat floor markers (`box` props, 7 x 0.3 x 7 cm): a light grey center marker and three orange markers 30 cm to the left, 30 cm forward and 30 cm to the right of it. The figure faces +X (forward) the whole time and never turns. Markers sit between the feet at mid-foot level.

**Stance and arms (held throughout).** Feet hip-width (ankles under the hip joints, 17.2 cm apart), toes straight ahead, trunk tall (about 1 to 3 degrees forward), eyes level and slightly down. Arms bent and held in front of the chest: upper arms hang at the sides, elbows about 117 degrees, forearms forward and slightly up, hands about 13 cm apart with palms facing in. The arms stay still relative to the trunk ("arms quiet").

**Pattern.** center, left, center, forward, center, right, center, two rounds per loop (12 hops in 6 s). Every hop takes off and lands on both feet together.

**One hop (0.5 s), measured from the bottom of a landing:**

| Key | Time in the hop | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| mid | 0.00 s | Bottom of the landing, knees about 32 degrees, both feet planted (heel 5 degrees off the floor) |
| push | 0.08 s | Legs extend, hips move about 3 cm toward the next marker, feet still planted |
| toe | 0.13 s | Toe-off: heels up (foot 22 degrees), knees about 5 degrees, weight released |
| lift, apex, reach | 0.17, 0.25, 0.33 s | Flight: ballistic hip height, toes pointed, knees tuck to about 18 degrees at the top; feet ease across to the next marker |
| touch | 0.37 s | Touchdown on the balls of the feet (foot 13 degrees heel-up), knees about 10 degrees; the IK target is already on the landing spot |
| land | 0.40 s | Weight 1, heels lower to 5 degrees, knees bending |
| mid | 0.50 s | Bottom of the next landing |

Contact 0.26 s, flight 0.24 s. The hips travel at constant speed through the air and decelerate and redirect on the ground; at the left, forward and right markers they reverse.

**Phases** (all `natural` breathing, research: breathe steadily, no breath hold):

| Phase id | Label | Time (s) | Cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| left1 | Hop left and back | 0-1 | c1 set-up, hop left and back |
| forward1 | Hop forward and back | 1-2 | c2 soft landing on the balls of the feet |
| right1 | Hop right and back | 2-3 | c3 face forward, knees over toes |
| left2 | Round 2: left and back | 3-4 | c4 small and quick |
| forward2 | Round 2: forward and back | 4-5 | c5 feet together, trunk tall |
| right2 | Round 2: right and back | 5-6 | c6 breathe steadily, keep the rhythm |

**Seamless loop.** The loop starts and ends at the bottom of a center landing (identical pose `mid_C` at 0 s and 6 s). Head displacement across the wrap is 0.03 cm per 5 ms step, against up to 0.99 cm per step in the air. The only visible effect of the zero-slope ends of the monotone-cubic tracks is a slightly slower sideways drift through that one center contact (it is the only contact where the hips keep moving the same way, from the right marker through center to the left marker).

**Key values (rig) vs research.**

| Measure | Animation | Research |
|---|---|---|
| Knee flexion at touchdown | about 10 degrees | about 9-13 degrees at contact (Sinsurin 2015, single-leg step-off landings) |
| Knee flexion at the lowest point | about 32 degrees (22 degrees of bend after contact) | knee amplitude about 20 degrees in repeated two-leg hopping (Kovacs 2021) |
| Foot at contact | ball of the foot, heel up 13 degrees (ankle about 6 degrees plantarflexion), heel lowers to 5 degrees (ankle about 11 degrees dorsiflexion): 17 degrees ankle excursion | land on the balls of the feet, heels lower slightly; excursion about 19-22 degrees at 2.2 Hz (Birch 2025) |
| Hop distance | 30 cm | about 20-40 cm (estimate; no study of this drill) |
| Hop rate | 2 per second | preferred about 2.2 per second (Farley 1991); videos.md estimate about 2 per second |
| Ground contact | 260 ms | 236-268 ms at 2.2 Hz (Hobara 2015, Birch 2025) |
| Flight | 240 ms; hips about 6 cm above take-off height, feet about 9 cm off the floor at the top | no published height; "small hops" |
| Trunk | about 3 degrees forward at the bottom of a landing, about 1 degree in the air; no rotation | tall and quiet, facing forward (hexagon test protocol) |

**Muscles** (rig ids only). Target: calves, quads. Synergists: glute_max, hamstrings, hip_flexors. Stabilizers: glute_med, adductors, core, spinal_extensors. Calves, quads, glute_max, hamstrings and hip_flexors curves are generated by the author script in sync with each hop (calves peak at push-off, quads at the bottom of the landing, hip flexors in the air); glute_med and adductors get a boost on hops that involve the side markers. Core and spinal extensors are steady (meta file). All curves are illustrative, not EMG.

**Checks.** `plantedContacts: [leg_r, leg_l]`, peak `rootTravel` 25-40 cm (hop distance range from videos.md and research.md), floor tolerance 0.5 cm. No still joints (the whole body travels). The author script also prints knee angles, foot clearance, travel and the head step at the loop seam.

## Decisions on uncertain points

1. **Directions (main open question).** reference.md could not see the directions (orbiting camera) and guessed "forward, to the side and back". research.md suggested a hexagon-test style pattern (center, forward, center, side, center, back, center). Followed videos.md as instructed: left, forward and right with a return to center after each point, facing forward the whole time, no backward hop. Basis: the JustGains "Standing 3-Point Hop" page (the only written source naming the directions), the GymVisual plank "3 point" sibling drills (left, center, right), and the 4 Corner Hops video (always back to the start box). Confidence stays medium-low; this is recorded in `exercise.reference.note`.
2. **Two feet, not one.** JustGains says "land on one foot"; GymVisual shows two-foot hops. Followed GymVisual (two feet together on every take-off and landing).
3. **Order and sides.** Authored side is `right` with the pattern going left first (videos.md order: center, left, center, forward, center, right, center). The `_left` clip and the player's Left switch mirror it, so that version goes right first. The pattern is otherwise symmetric.
4. **Distance and tempo.** 30 cm per hop (middle of the 25-40 cm estimate) and 2 hops per second (videos.md; research preferred rate 2.2 per second). Contact and flight split chosen inside the published contact-time range.
5. **Start position.** reference.md starts with arms relaxed at the sides; the loop is continuous hopping (no standing set-up), so the arms are already up in front of the chest from the first frame and the set-up is described in cue c1. This keeps the loop seamless.
6. **Ankle at contact.** Birch 2025 reports about 10 degrees dorsiflexion at contact for hopping in place; the mistakes and cues ask for landing on the balls of the feet with the heels lowering slightly. The animation lands on the balls of the feet (ankle about 6 degrees plantarflexion at touchdown) and reaches about 11 degrees dorsiflexion at the bottom, so its excursion (17 degrees) is a little under the 19-22 degree range.
7. **Floor markers.** research.md lists optional tape or dots on the floor. Drawn as thin `box` props because the schema has no flat dot or tape kind.

## Known limitations

- **Framing crops the head.** The player frames from joint positions only, so in the default presets the top of the head is cut off for this tall, small-footprint exercise, worst in the air. The animation itself is complete (seen with the zoomed-out inspection render).
- The weight blend at touchdown dips the foot up to about 0.2 cm below the floor for a few milliseconds (within the 0.5 cm tolerance).
- Planted feet use one fixed heel-up angle (5 degrees), so the "heels lower slightly" part of the landing happens during the touchdown blend (13 to 5 degrees), not while fully planted.
- Hands are the rig's flat hand shape, so "loose fists" read as flat hands with palms facing in.

## Needs from platform

- **Player framing (src/player.js, `loadExercise`):** include body shape extents (head top, feet) in the framing box, or fit the camera distance to the box height as well as its diagonal. Standing exercises with a small footprint, like this one, get the head cropped in every preset.
- **Optional:** a flat floor-marker prop kind (dot or tape strip) so markers do not need to be drawn as thin boxes.

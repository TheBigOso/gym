## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** standing tall, feet hip-width, arms relaxed at the sides; the video rotates its view between front and side.
2. **Movement:** small two-foot hops to three points around a center (for example forward, to the side and back), landing softly and returning toward center.
3. **Hop mechanics:** slight knee bend on landing, quick rebound, arms bent and held in front of the chest during the hops.
4. **Loop:** continuous hopping sequence.
5. **Highlights:** quadriceps, calves, hip flexors and core.

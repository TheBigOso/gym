Seen and rejected: "Triangle Jump" by HomeBodyTraining (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HuWc36Ay_k8). It is a plank drill: the feet jump to the left at about 45 degrees, back to center, then to the right. It is not our standing exercise, but it suggests the "3 point" family of drills (Plank 3 Point Hops, Elbow Plank 3 Point Hops, Push Up 3 Points Hops, all GymVisual names) treats the three points as left, center and right.

## Pattern clarification

The best written source for the GymVisual exercise itself is the JustGains page "Standing 3-Point Hop" (https://justgains.com/e/3-point-standing-hops), whose URL uses the GymVisual name. It describes **three points: left, forward and right, hopped in a triangle pattern**, cycling left, forward, right. Its text also says to "jump off both feet and land on one foot". That conflicts with the GymVisual reference (two-foot hops), reads like auto-generated copy, and should not be followed.

Recommended pattern for the animation, combining that source with our reference.md:

- **The three points:** left, forward and right of the start spot, in a fan or "T" in front of and beside the body. The athlete keeps facing forward the whole time and never turns. Backward is not one of the points. (reference.md guessed "forward, to the side and back", but no source supports a backward hop.)
- **Distance:** small hops, roughly 25-40 cm (about one to one and a half foot lengths) from center. This is our own estimate for "small hops"; no source gives a number.
- **Two-foot vs one-foot:** two feet, hip-width, taking off and landing together, as the GymVisual reference shows.
- **Return to center:** yes. Hop out to a point, hop back to center, then on to the next point: center, left, center, forward, center, right, center, and repeat. reference.md notes the GymVisual model "returning toward center". The plank sibling drills (left, center, right) and the 4 Corner Hops video, which always comes back to the start box, also fit this.
- **Tempo:** continuous and rhythmic, with a short ground contact and a quick rebound. About 2 hops per second, so one full cycle of 6 hops takes about 3 seconds. Knees bend slightly on each landing and the arms stay bent in front of the chest (reference.md).

## Confidence

Confidence: medium-low that the directions are left, forward and right with a return to center. Only one written source names the directions (JustGains), and it has errors elsewhere. High that the hops are two-footed, small and continuous, since the GymVisual reference says so. No video's pattern was confirmed frame by frame.

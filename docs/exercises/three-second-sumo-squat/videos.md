## Pattern clarification

- **Stance width:** wide, about 1.5x shoulder width (GymVisual). The demos say "wide" or "wider than shoulder width". The Travis Tarrant cue is a good limit for the animation: if the knees would cave inward, the stance is too wide.
- **Toe angle:** turned out about 30-45 degrees (GymVisual). Fit With Melanie says "toes pointed out" with no angle. Knees should track in line with the toes all the way down.
- **Depth:** about thighs parallel to the floor (GymVisual). Travis Tarrant says "nearly parallel". Hips sit straight down between the heels with the trunk upright, rather than a deep hip hinge.
- **Where the 3 seconds go:** the lowering (eccentric) phase. This matches the reference note and the closest demo (Emma Terrazas: 3 seconds down). The 3 seconds are not a pause. At the bottom, GymVisual shows only a brief pause; the Emma Terrazas 3-2-1 variant holds for 2 seconds, which we should not copy. The stand-up is at normal pace, about 1 second (Fit With Melanie and Emma Terrazas both stand up in 1 second). Suggested animation tempo: 3 s down, about 0.3-0.5 s pause, about 1 s up.
- **Hand position:** hands clasped at the chest (GymVisual). None of the video descriptions specify hands. Clasped hands at the chest is the usual bodyweight choice and keeps the trunk upright, so keep the GymVisual position.
- **Load:** body weight only (GymVisual data sheet). The tempo-specific demos do not say whether a weight is used; ignore any dumbbell or kettlebell they show.

## Confidence

Medium. The tempo (3-second lowering, brief pause, normal-speed stand) and the stance cues are confirmed by video descriptions from two tempo-specific clips and two technique tutorials. However, I could not watch any frames, only one source is a credentialed physical therapist (and its description gives no tempo), and no clip matches GymVisual exactly (3 s down, brief pause, hands at chest, body weight). Hand position and toe angle rest on the GymVisual reference alone.

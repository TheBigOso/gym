## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** wide stance, about 1.5x shoulder width, toes turned out about 30-45°, hands clasped at the chest. Front view.
2. **Down phase:** slow three-second descent: hips sit down between the heels, knees track over the toes, trunk upright, to about thighs-parallel.
3. **Bottom:** brief pause.
4. **Up phase:** stand back up at a normal pace.
5. **Laterality:** bilateral, symmetric.
6. **Highlights:** quadriceps, adductors and glutes.

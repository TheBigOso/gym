## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** lying supine, knees bent about 90°, feet flat on the floor hip-width, hands behind the head with elbows wide.
2. **Up phase:** curl the head, shoulders and trunk off the floor, then continue until the trunk is about three-quarters of the way to upright (roughly 60-70° from the floor); feet stay down.
3. **Down phase:** lower under control back to the floor.
4. **Laterality:** bilateral, symmetric.
5. **Highlights:** rectus abdominis (and obliques).

## Notes for the video, research and integrate tickets

Elbows stay wide; the hands should not pull the head. Confirm the end angle that defines "3/4" against demos and research.

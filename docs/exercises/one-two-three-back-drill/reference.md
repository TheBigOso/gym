## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Setup:** three cones in a triangle: one at the back (apex) and two at the front corners. Elevated wide shot; the figure is small in frame.
2. **Pattern:** the athlete starts at the apex cone, runs to one front cone, shuffles/backpedals across or diagonally, and returns to the apex: a 1-2-3 cone sequence with a backward segment.
3. **Locomotion:** short sprint steps forward, lateral shuffle and backpedal segments; athletic posture, arms pumping.
4. **Loop:** repeats the triangle continuously.
5. **Highlights:** quadriceps and glutes.

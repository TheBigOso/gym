## Pattern clarification

Assembled from the GymVisual movement description (reference.md), the FitWill written guide for the same GymVisual-licensed exercise ("3 Leg Chatarunga Pose", https://fitwill.app/exercise/5915/3-leg-chatarunga-pose/), Yoga Journal's Chaturanga Dandasana guide (https://www.yogajournal.com/article/beginners/chaturanga-dandasana/), and Alo Moves' "Eka Pada Push-Ups" article (https://blog.alomoves.com/movement/fun-pose-friday-eka-pada-push-ups), not from the videos.

1. **Order of positions:** high plank, then downward-facing dog (hips up and back), then three-legged dog (one leg lifts straight up behind, hips kept roughly level), then shift forward over the hands into a three-legged plank, then lower to chaturanga, then press back to plank. Alternate legs between reps.
2. **Does the lifted leg stay up while lowering?** Yes. FitWill's step reads "Bend your elbows to lower into a Chaturanga position while keeping your lifted leg in the air", and the leg comes down only as you press back up to plank. Alo Moves' related one-leg push-up keeps the leg up for the full set. In the animation the raised leg should stay lifted (roughly in line with the body or a little above) through the whole lowering, and the hips should not open or twist toward the lifted side.
3. **Elbow position:** elbows hug in against the ribs and point back, not out. Lower until the upper arms are about parallel to the floor, with the elbows near 90 degrees (Yoga Journal: "Create 90-degree angles with your arms, with your upper arms parallel to the floor and forearms perpendicular"). Shoulders should not dip below elbow height.
4. **Breath cues:** the usual vinyasa cueing is to inhale into three-legged dog (lifting the leg), inhale or hold while shifting forward to plank, **exhale to lower** into chaturanga (Yoga Journal: "As you exhale, bend your elbows, keeping them drawn in against your sides"), then inhale to press back up or move on. FitWill's guide is inconsistent: one line says exhale on the way down, another says exhale on the press up. For the app, "exhale to lower" is the standard yoga cue.

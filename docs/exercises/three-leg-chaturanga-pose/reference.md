## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** high plank, hands under shoulders, body in one line. Side view.
2. **Phase 1:** push hips up and back into downward-facing dog.
3. **Phase 2:** lift one leg straight up behind (three-legged dog), hips staying level-ish.
4. **Phase 3:** shift forward over the hands and lower into chaturanga (low plank, elbows bent close to the ribs) while keeping the raised leg lifted.
5. **Return:** back to plank and repeat; alternate legs between reps.
6. **Highlights:** triceps, hamstrings/glutes of the lifted leg, shoulders.

## Notes for the video, research and integrate tickets

Close relative of 3 Leg Dog Pose (#7); the difference is the knee-to-chaturanga lowering. Confirm whether the lifted leg stays up during the lowering in demos.

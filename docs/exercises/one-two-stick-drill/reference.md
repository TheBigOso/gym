## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** athletic stance at one end of a row of 5-6 low markers laid on the floor about 30-40 cm apart, feet hip-width, knees and hips softly bent (quarter squat), trunk slightly forward, hands clasped in front of the chest. Viewed from the front.
2. **Movement:** quick lateral footwork down the row: two fast foot contacts ("1-2") between each pair of markers, lead foot then trail foot, moving sideways without crossing the feet.
3. **Path:** travels laterally along the row to the far end; the reference loops (likely back the other way).
4. **Posture throughout:** stays low and square to the front, chest up, arms fixed at the chest; light, quick ground contacts on the balls of the feet.
5. **Highlights in the reference:** quadriceps and calves.

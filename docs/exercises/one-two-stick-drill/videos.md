## Pattern clarification

- **What "stick" probably means.** All three videos with "1-2 stick" in the title or description talk about single-leg stability, balance, or holding a position. The common performance-coaching meaning is: take two quick steps ("1-2"), then **stick** (freeze) the landing on one leg. The GymVisual data sheet lists the equipment as "Stick", and our reference read the drawn markers as short sticks on the floor. That may be GymVisual's interpretation and not the usual coaching meaning. **Flag: confirm against the GymVisual GIF whether the model ever pauses on one leg.** If the GIF shows only continuous two-foot footwork over floor markers, it matches the SKLZ-style lateral shuffle more closely than the coaching "1-2 stick".
- **Footwork.** The videos agree that the movement is lateral (sideways) and does not cross the feet. The cues are to press off the outside (trail) foot, contact on the ball of the foot, keep the shin angle up (shins not collapsing), and keep the chest up. A 1-2 rhythm (lead foot, then trail foot) is consistent with GymVisual's "two contacts per gap".
- **Spacing.** No source gives a distance. GymVisual's 30-40 cm marker spacing is still unconfirmed. In ladder-style versions, the spacing is roughly one ladder square (about 40-45 cm).
- **Tempo.** The Adirondack Club video says to start slowly (marching) and gradually speed up. The Restored Strength video frames the drill as a speed drill. So tempo is a progression, from a marching pace to quick contacts. GymVisual shows the fast version.
- **Direction changes.** No description states the turnaround. The Morrill title shows that a forward-moving variant exists. Most likely the drill alternates left and right, either one rep each way or down the row and back. That fits GymVisual's loop back the other way, but this is **unconfirmed**.
- **Arms.** The Adirondack Club video cues opposite arm and leg action. GymVisual shows the hands clasped at the chest. **Flag: the arm position differs between sources.**

## Confidence

Low to moderate. The URLs and metadata are real and verified from the watch pages, but no frames or transcripts could be checked. The Restored Strength video is the best name match. Whether GymVisual's "1 2 Stick Drill" includes a single-leg stick (hold), or is only continuous lateral footwork over floor sticks, is still unresolved and needs a visual check.

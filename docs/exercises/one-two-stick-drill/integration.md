# 1 2 Stick Drill: integration notes

Issue #4 (Batch 1, exercise 1 of 10). Movement reference: GymVisual #596513 (see `reference.md`). Built 2026-11-26.

Files: `build/meta/one-two-stick-drill.json`, `build/author-one-two-stick-drill.js`, `src/exercises/one-two-stick-drill.json`, `exports/one-two-stick-drill.glb` (one clip, bilateral).

## What was built

**Layout.** Five yellow floor sticks (`stick` props, 60 cm long, 2.5 cm diameter) lie across the line of travel at z = -80, -40, 0, 40, 80 cm, so the gaps are 40 cm wide. The figure faces +X (the front) and travels sideways along Z: it starts in an athletic stance outside the left end of the row, works through the 4 gaps and finishes outside the right end, then comes back.

**Stance (held throughout).** Feet 18 cm apart (hip-width) when together, on the balls of the feet (planted foot pitched 10 degrees heel-up), pelvis tipped forward 25 degrees with the chest kept up, hands clasped in front of the chest (fingers interlaced, elbows down and slightly out), head level and eyes forward.

**Footwork.** Each move into the next gap is two contacts, "1-2": the lead foot steps 40 cm over the stick into the gap, then the trail foot follows to land 18 cm beside it. Feet never cross (closest ankle spacing 18 cm). Each step: push (planted), lift (foot off, weight 0, about 4 cm up), clear (over the stick, about 9 cm ankle lift), reach, touch-down (target already on its final spot, weight 0.85), land (weight 1). The pelvis follows the midpoint of the feet, with a small dip of 0.8 cm on each landing.

| Phase id | Label | Time (s) | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| setup | Athletic stance | 0.0-0.8 | Hold the stance outside the left end |
| quickRight | 1-2 quick feet right | 0.8-3.6 | 10 contacts (4 gaps plus out), right foot leads; 0.28 s per contact |
| stopRight | Stop | 3.6-4.2 | Stop under control, small settle (1.6 cm), reset |
| quickLeft | 1-2 quick feet left | 4.2-7.0 | 10 contacts back, left foot leads |
| stopLeft | Stop and reset | 7.0-8.0 | Stop, settle, back to the start pose (seamless loop) |

All phases use natural breathing (research: breathe rhythmically, do not hold the breath).

**Muscles** (rig ids only). Target: quads, calves. Synergists: glute_med, adductors, glute_max, hip_flexors. Stabilizers: core, hamstrings, spinal_extensors, ant_deltoid. Calves and quads activation is generated by the author script in sync with the contacts (planted leg peaks while the other foot is in the air, lower on the swing leg); the other curves are in the meta file. All curves are illustrative, not EMG.

**Checks.** `plantedContacts: [leg_r, leg_l]`, peak `rootTravel` 150-205 cm (5 moves at the research spacing of 30-40 cm gives 150-200 cm), floor tolerance 0.5 cm. No still joints (the whole body travels). The author script also checks stick clearance and that the feet never cross, and fails if either is broken.

## Decisions on uncertain points

1. **Which movement the name means.** Fitwill and the videos with "1-2 stick" in the title use "stick" to mean holding a one-leg landing (step-and-hold). The GymVisual data sheet says equipment "Stick" and the reference shows continuous lateral quick feet over floor markers. Per the issue, this module follows GymVisual: continuous footwork, no single-leg hold, sticks drawn as `stick` props. The naming caution is also recorded in `exercise.reference.note`.
2. **Spacing and count.** 5 sticks (reference: 5-6) at 40 cm, the top of the reference estimate and just under ladder-square size, so both feet fit in a gap at hip-width with room to spare.
3. **Loop.** The reference loops, "likely back the other way" (unconfirmed by videos). Chosen: down the row leading with the right foot, short stop, back leading with the left. This makes the full loop mirror-symmetric, so `authoredSide` is `bilateral`.
4. **Arms.** GymVisual shows hands clasped at the chest; other agility sources cue arm pumping. Followed GymVisual.
5. **Feet.** Contacts on the balls of the feet (GymVisual, Fitwill) are shown as a 10 degree heel-up foot while planted. Feet point straight ahead (no toe-out).
6. **Tempo.** Videos describe a progression from marching to fast. The animation shows a quick but readable pace (0.28 s per contact); the prescription text covers the slow start.
7. **Hip flexors** are listed as synergists for lifting the foot over the sticks: our inference from the movement, flagged as such in `muscleNotes` (the research does not name them).

## Known limitations

- Muscle activation is illustrative, not EMG.
- Stick clearance is checked only by the author script (foot ellipsoid vs stick height near each stick); verify.mjs has no prop collision check.
- Feet point straight ahead; no toe-out or ankle inversion/eversion (the rig ankle has dorsiflexion only).
- The trunk is held still relative to the pelvis (as cued); no small counter-sway.
- The module is about 300 KB pretty-printed (124 complete poses, one per key). It could be trimmed by moving to keyframe `set` overrides, at the cost of readability.
- GLB is baked at 30 fps, so each 0.2 s swing has about 6 samples.

## Needs from platform

1. **verify.mjs slide check reads the wrong field.** It tests `prev[id].w`, which is always undefined, instead of `prev[id].weight`. So whenever a contact reaches weight 1, any target change since the previous sample (taken while the weight was still below 1) counts as a slide. Worked around here with touch-down keys (target on its final spot while the weight ramps from 0.85 to 1). Fix: use `prev[id].weight`.
2. **Stale rotation arc in the player.** When an exercise without a `rotationArc` guide is loaded after one with it, the arc, band and "N° rotation" label from the previous exercise stay on screen (visible in every screenshot of this drill). Hide `arcLine`, `bandMesh`, `refLine` and `labels.arc` when there is no `rotationArc` guide.
3. **HUD is hard-coded for rotation drills.** It always shows thoracic/lumbar rotation, shoulder-line tilt, neck rotation and support elbow. For this drill knees, hips, hip travel and foot clearance would be useful. Suggest a per-module HUD list (for example `hud: ["knee", "hip", "rootTravel"]`).
4. **Guides.** The player draws only the first `path` guide (the pelvis path here is ignored), does not render `jointAngle` guides, and shows the `gaze` guide only in phases named `rotate` or `hold`.
5. **Shots script.** `__rr.load(i)` does not update the exercise tab highlight (tab A stays selected in full-page screenshots of exercise C).
6. **Optional lib helpers.** `fitLeg` (leg channels that reproduce an IK foot placement, so weight blends are seamless) and the step schedule (push, lift, clear, reach, touch, land) in `build/author-one-two-stick-drill.js` would be reusable for other travelling footwork drills if moved into `build/lib.js`.

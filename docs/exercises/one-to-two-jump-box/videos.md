## Pattern clarification

- **Takeoff leg:** both matching descriptions say to take off from one leg. Neither names left or right. Signum says to train both legs equally, so reps should alternate the takeoff leg or be done on each side. GymVisual adds a step into a staggered stance before takeoff. Signum starts from a still stance on one leg instead (lift one foot, swing the arms, jump). That is a small difference in the start, not in the pattern.
- **Box height:** none of the descriptions gives a number. Signum says to start at a "comfortable height" and stand 12 to 24 in back from the box. This fits GymVisual's roughly knee-height box but does not confirm it.
- **Landing position:** a soft landing on both feet on top of the box (OPEX: "land on both legs"; Signum: "land softly with both feet on the box"), then reset on top. This matches GymVisual's two-foot quarter-to-half squat landing.
- **Stepping down:** OPEX and Signum both say to step back down, not jump down. This matches GymVisual's step-down return.

**Confidence:** medium. Two descriptions (OPEX Fitness, Signum) clearly state the single-leg takeoff, two-foot landing and step-down. The Sean Buckley match rests on the title only, and the RAWR Strength landing is unconfirmed. No frames were watched, and none of the presenters is a confirmed licensed clinician.

## Movement, phase by phase

1. **Start:** standing tall beside/behind a plyo box (about knee height), arms at the sides. Side view.
2. **Takeoff:** step into a staggered stance and drive off **one leg** (single-leg takeoff), arms swinging forward, trunk inclined.
3. **Flight:** both legs come up together.
4. **Landing:** **two-foot** landing on top of the box in a quarter-to-half squat, hips back, knees over toes, arms forward for balance.
5. **Return:** stand up on the box and step back down (not shown as a jump down), reset to standing.
